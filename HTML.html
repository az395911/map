Auckland Aquarium Interactive Map

1

Scott's Hut

Step into the world of Antarctic explorers. Experience a faithful replica of Captain Robert Scott's hut from his 1911 expedition to the South Pole.

2

White Out

Feel the chill of Antarctica in this immersive snow room. Bundle up and experience what it's like in sub-zero temperatures!

3

Antarctic Ice Adventure

Journey through the frozen wonderland of Antarctica and learn about the incredible creatures that call it home.

4

Penguin Ice Caves

Watch the adorable King and Gentoo penguins waddle, swim, and play in their icy domain. A true highlight of any visit!

5

1985 Memories

Discover the fascinating history of Kelly Tarlton's vision and how this world-famous aquarium came to life in 1985.

6

Jellyfish

Be mesmerized by the graceful dance of jellyfish in a stunning display of colour and movement. Truly hypnotic!

7

Art Aquarium

Where art meets marine life! Explore beautiful artworks inspired by the ocean and its incredible inhabitants.

8

Shipwreck Shores Playground

A fun-filled play area for kids themed around a shipwreck adventure. Perfect for little explorers to burn off energy!

9

Underwater VR Experience

Dive into a virtual underwater world without getting wet! An immersive VR adventure like no other.

10

Turtle Bay

Meet the magnificent sea turtles as they glide gracefully through the water. Learn about conservation efforts to protect them.

11

Turtle Rescue

Learn about the incredible rescue and rehabilitation work done to save injured sea turtles and return them to the ocean.

12

Shark Zone

Get up close with the ocean's most feared predators. Walk through the tunnel as sharks glide overhead — an unforgettable experience!

13

Shipwreck Discovery

Explore a sunken shipwreck teeming with marine life. Discover the creatures that have made this wreck their home.

14

Sea Cave Adventure

Venture into mysterious sea caves and uncover the hidden wonders of the deep ocean in this exciting exploration zone.

15

Seahorse and Friends

Marvel at the delicate beauty of seahorses and their ocean friends. A magical encounter with some of the sea's most unique creatures.
